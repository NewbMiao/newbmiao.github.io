Ring buffer — counter pointers and mirror indexing

聊聊ringbuffer

A ring buffer (circular queue) joins head and tail: read/write cursors advance around a fixed slice instead of shifting elements on every dequeue.

The payoff: after a dequeue you do not slide the rest of the queue forward; the next enqueue overwrites the freed slot.

Below is a common pattern—separate read/write counters—with a diagram-friendly walkthrough. Code is adapted from chanx ringbuffer.

ringbuffer，是环形缓存区, 或叫环形队列。

不同于一般常见的队列，环形队列收尾相连，通过移动指针来控制队列中内容的读写。
